👑 Organizer Workflow
Admin Code Protected
  1. Verify attendee's manual bank transfer / screenshot payment.
  2. Open the Pass Generator and enter their Name, Email, and WhatsApp.
  3. Pass is recorded in Google Sheets, QR is automatically emailed, and a pre-filled WhatsApp link is generated.
  4. Click the WhatsApp link and attach the generated ticket image to notify the attendee instantly!

📱 Door Staff Workflow
Zero Login Needed
  1. Open the Door Scanner URL on any smartphone browser (iOS or Android).
  2. Allow browser camera permission when prompted.
  3. Point camera at the attendee's QR pass (digital on phone or printed).
  4. System gives instant audio, haptic, and visual feedback (Valid / Already Used / Invalid) and records timestamps live in Google Sheets.
